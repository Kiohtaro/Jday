A Little Letter for You

To Jai,

HAPPY BIRTHDAY!!!

Where do I begin? AHAHAHA andami ko gusto sabihin, but I don’t think a single letter would be enough to put them into words. You still probably hate me, but that is fine, I still wanted to do something special for your bday. I've been thinking kung ano ibibigay ko sayo for your bday, I had something other than this, but due to the constricts of time, sa December ko nalang ibibigay AHAHAHAH.

First, I hope you know how much I appreciate you. I hope you never lose that kindness you show to everyone and the way you're someone people can genuinely rely on. These are some of the things that I really admire about you. I hope you never forget how much of a difference you make in the lives of the people around you.

Sometimes, I still find myself looking back at the little things we used to do. Yung mga gabi na we’d spend hours sending each other reels, reacting to them, and finding ways to tease each other. The memories of completing It takes two and how you introduced me to hiking games in roblox. Alam ko na these might seem like ordinary things, but they're memories I hold close to my heart. I never really thought that something as simple as spending time together could become something I'd look back on with this much fondness.

I miss those moments. I miss how easy things used to feel between us, and alam ko naman na things aren’t the same anymore, but I'm still thankful that I got to experience those things with you. Its something I woudnt want to change about my past, the chance to know you and create those memories with you.

I also want you to know that I've come to understand something. Na kahit gaano ko ka gusto na mag work yung between us, I know I have no right to keep asking you to hold on, to keep trying, or to keep fighting for something when you’ve already been feeling tired for a while. I understand now that wanting something to work doesn’t automatically mean both people have to keep trying until it does, and kahit its super difficult for me to accept that, di ko rin gusto na it becomes something na nagpapahirap sayo.

I wont pretend that my feelings have simply disappeared, because honestly, they haven’t. You still hold a very special place in my heart, and I really don’t think that will change just because things between us have. I may not know what place I'll have in your life moving forward, but know that you'll always have a piece of my heart. A piece that will always be grateful for you, for the memories we made, and for the person you allowed me to know.

I hope life treats you kindly. I pray that you get to accomplish everything you’ve been working so hard for, that you get to visit all the places you want to visit in the future, and that you find happiness even in the smallest things. I pray that you continue to meet people who appreciate your kindness, who understands your heart, and remind you that deserve love and care.

And whenever life gets difficult, I pray you remember that you're someone capable of getting through more than you think, and I pray you never feel like you have to carry everything on your own. God will always be there for you when you need someone to run to (tas ako din syempre :> AHHAHA). I will always be someone who genuinely cares for you.

I don’t know what the future holds for us, and I don’t want to make promises or expectations out of a day that’s supposed to be about you. I just wanted you to know that somewhere along the way, you became someone incredibly important to me, and I'll always treasure how our paths crossed.

Thank you, jaiii, for the late night conversations, the random reels, the games, the little moments that became some of my favorite memories. I'll never get tired of thanking you for being someone I got to know this closely, I'll always be grateful for that.

I hope this birthday is filled with the happiness you deserve. Keep being the person that you are, and never lose the things that make you, you!

Happy birthday again! You will always be someone I'm glad to have found.

Always, with love,
Ian
